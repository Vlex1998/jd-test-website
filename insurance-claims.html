Is Your Adjuster Experienced Enough?

It's surprisingly common for insurance companies to send adjusters who lack roofing expertise to handle roof damage claims. This leads to problems — including misidentifying hail damage as heat-related wear (which isn't covered).

During major storm events, insurance companies even bring in adjusters from unrelated departments with zero roofing experience. We've encountered adjusters who struggled to climb a ladder. Having a knowledgeable professional in your corner makes all the difference.

FAQ

Insurance Claims FAQ

Is the insurance claims process difficult?

The process can be tricky, but we handle everything for you — from filing the claim to negotiating with the insurer. You focus on your family; we focus on getting you a fair settlement.

What if my insurance claim is denied?

Don't give up. We'll review your policy, gather additional evidence, and advocate on your behalf until we reach a resolution. Our team won't stop until your roof is repaired or replaced.

What happens after my claim is approved?

We get to work immediately. Our experienced crew repairs or replaces your roof quickly and to the highest standards, ensuring the work matches or exceeds insurance scope requirements.

Do insurance companies cover full roof replacements?

It depends on your policy and the extent of damage. Some policies cover the full replacement cost; others cover partial repairs. We review your policy with you upfront so you know exactly what to expect.

How much will I pay out of pocket?

Most of our storm damage customers pay only their deductible. We work to ensure the insurance payout covers the full scope of your repair or replacement.
