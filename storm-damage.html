Types of Storm Damage

We Handle Every Type of Storm

Houston faces some of the most severe weather in the country. Our team is trained for every scenario.

Hail Storm Damage

Hailstones of any size cause dents and granule loss in shingles, compromising their protective layer. Even pea-sized hail can accumulate enough damage to require professional repair. Small hail = big problems over time.

Hurricane & Wind Damage

Hurricane-force winds can lift, crack, or completely remove shingle sections. A professional post-hurricane inspection is essential — hidden damage can lead to major leaks in subsequent rains.

Tornado Damage

Tornadoes create destructive winds and flying debris capable of tearing apart entire roof sections. Our crews are trained for rapid response to tornado-damaged properties across the Houston metro.

Wind Storm Damage

Even non-hurricane winds lift and displace shingles, creating vulnerabilities that allow water to infiltrate. If shingles are missing or lifted after any windstorm, call us for a free inspection.

Know the Signs

Hail Damage Indicators

The aftermath of a hailstorm can be deceptive — what looks minor from the ground can be extensive on your roof. Look for these warning signs:

Random damage with no discernible pattern across the roof surface
Granule loss exposing the felt underlayment (black patches on shingles)
Soft-touch dents resembling bruises on an apple when pressed
Dents in gutters, downspouts, window sills, or AC units
Cracked or broken window screens, siding chips, or deck damage
