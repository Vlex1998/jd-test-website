Understanding Asphalt Shingles

Asphalt shingles are the most widely used roofing material in the Houston area. They're built from a fiberglass base coated with asphalt and topped with mineral granules for protection and color, and they come in three tiers — 3-tab, architectural, and designer.

3-Tab Shingles

Also called strip shingles, 3-tab shingles are cut into three uniform tabs and lay flat across the roof for a clean, traditional look. They were once the industry standard for budget-friendly residential roofing.

Pros

  • Lower Upfront Cost: the least expensive option, popular with budget-conscious homeowners.
  • Simplicity: lightweight and straightforward to install.
  • Classic Look: a clean, uniform appearance, though color options are limited.

Cons

  • Shorter Lifespan: typically lasts 15–20 years, shorter than dimensional shingles.
  • Lower Durability: more susceptible to wind and hail damage, leading to frequent repairs.
  • Limited Aesthetic Appeal: a flatter, less dimensional look than architectural or designer shingles.

Our Approach

We prioritize quality and durability, so we don't install new 3-tab roofs — the shorter lifespan doesn't hold up well against Houston's heat, humidity, and storms. We're happy to repair an existing 3-tab roof, but for a full replacement we'll always recommend moving up to architectural or designer shingles.

Architectural Shingles

Also known as dimensional or laminate shingles, architectural shingles are built from multiple layers fused together, creating a thicker, more textured profile that mimics the look of wood shake or slate.

Pros

  • Enhanced Durability: typically lasts 25–30 years, depending on maintenance and climate.
  • Stronger Wind & Impact Resistance: holds up better to Houston's storms and hail than 3-tab shingles.
  • Aesthetic Variety: a wide range of colors and textures to match your home's style.

Cons

  • Higher Cost: more expensive up front than 3-tab shingles.
  • Heavier Weight: requires proper decking and structural support.

Designer Shingles

Designer, or premium, shingles sit at the top of the asphalt shingle category. They're engineered for maximum durability and dramatic curb appeal, often replicating the look of natural slate or cedar shake.

Pros

  • Exceptional Aesthetic Appeal: unique styles and colors for a luxurious, high-end look.
  • Superior Durability: the best resistance to wind, hail, and UV exposure.
  • Comprehensive Warranties: often backed by the strongest manufacturer coverage available.

Cons

  • Premium Cost: the most expensive asphalt shingle option, reflecting the quality and design.
  • Complex Installation: requires skilled, certified installers to ensure proper performance.

Key Considerations When Choosing Shingles

Weather Resistance and Durability

Houston sees extreme heat, humidity, hail, and hurricane-force wind, so durability isn't optional. Impact-resistant shingles are essential in hail-prone areas, and wind-resistant shingles matter for our storm season. GAF, CertainTeed, and Owens Corning all offer enhanced wind warranties when a certified contractor handles the installation.

Aesthetic Preferences

Your home's architectural style and your personal taste should drive the final decision. Designer shingles offer the most variety for a customized look, architectural shingles provide a broad range of colors and textures, and 3-tab shingles are more basic in appearance.

Budget and Longevity

3-tab shingles are the most affordable upfront, but architectural and designer shingles deliver better long-term value through durability and curb appeal. It's worth weighing the initial cost against potential savings in maintenance and how long the roof will actually last. Certifications and Warranties

Manufacturer certifications aren't just a logo on a truck — they determine what warranty coverage you actually qualify for.

Certification Programs

  • GAF Certified Contractor: vetted for proper installation practices, unlocking enhanced warranty coverage on GAF shingle systems beyond the standard material-only warranty.
  • CertainTeed Master Shingle Applicator: recognizes installers who meet CertainTeed's training and quality standards, required to access their strongest extended warranty options.
  • Owens Corning Preferred Contractor: a locally vetted, reputable, and properly insured contractor status, granting access to enhanced limited warranty tiers.

Warranty Options

  • Manufacturer Material Warranty: covers defects in the shingles themselves. Most manufacturers offer a limited lifetime warranty, with the strongest coverage reserved for certified installers.
  • Our Workmanship Warranty: JD Roofing Solutions backs every installation with a 10-year workmanship warranty, covering the quality of our labor separately from material defects.
  • Specialized Coverage: depending on the product line, additional coverage may be available for wind resistance, algae streak protection, and impact resistance.
