Pearland sits south of Houston, closer to the Gulf Coast than much of the metro area. That location brings more exposure to tropical weather, which should shape how you approach a roof replacement.

Wind Is the Big Concern

Tropical storms and hurricanes can bring strong, sustained winds to Pearland and Brazoria County. Wind damage usually starts at roof edges, corners, and ridges. When replacing your roof, consider:

  • Shingles with high wind ratings, installed using the manufacturer's high-wind nailing pattern.
  • Proper starter strips at the eaves and rakes to seal the first row.
  • Quality drip edge and flashing, firmly fastened. See our drip edge guide.
  • Synthetic underlayment and ice and water shield in leak-prone areas. See synthetic vs felt.

Heavy Rain and Drainage

Gulf storms dump heavy rain quickly. Clean, properly sized gutters and well-sealed valleys and penetrations are essential. See gutters and roof drainage.

Check Your Insurance

Review your wind and hail deductible before hurricane season. Depending on where you live, your coverage may have specific windstorm terms. Ask your agent and read our ACV vs RCV guide so you know what to expect if you need to file a claim.

Signs It Is Time for a New Roof

  • Missing or creased shingles after storms. See wind damage signs.
  • Leaks during heavy rain
  • Granule loss, curling, or cracking
  • A roof that is reaching the end of its expected life

Prepare Before Storm Season

Have your roof inspected each spring, fix small problems, trim trees, and take "before" photos. Our hurricane season roof checklist covers everything.
