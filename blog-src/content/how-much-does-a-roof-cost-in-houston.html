Roof replacement cost varies widely based on size, material, and roof complexity, so any number you see online should be treated as a general ballpark rather than a quote. Here's what actually drives the price up or down.

What Affects Your Roof's Cost

  • Roof size: priced by the "square" (100 square feet), so a larger roof costs more overall.
  • Material tier: 3-tab shingles are the most affordable upfront; architectural and designer shingles cost more but last longer and carry stronger warranties.
  • Roof pitch and complexity: steep roofs, multiple valleys, and dormers all increase labor time and cost.
  • Tear-off and disposal: removing and hauling away the old roof adds to the total.
  • Decking repair: if rotted plywood or OSB is found once the old roof comes off, replacing it adds cost.
  • Permits: local permitting fees vary by municipality within the Houston metro area.

General Cost Tiers

As a rough general guide, 3-tab shingle roofs tend to sit at the lower end of the cost range, architectural shingles in the middle (the most common choice for Houston homeowners), and designer shingles, metal, or tile at the higher end. Your actual price depends entirely on your roof's specific size and complexity, which is why we don't publish a flat number — it would be misleading either way.
