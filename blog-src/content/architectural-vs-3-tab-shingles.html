If you are getting a new asphalt shingle roof, you will likely choose between two main types: 3-tab shingles and architectural shingles (also called dimensional or laminated). They are made from similar materials but differ a lot in look, durability, and value.

3-Tab Shingles

3-tab shingles are a single flat layer with cutouts that make each shingle look like three separate tabs. They have a uniform, flat look.

  • Pros: lowest upfront cost.
  • Cons: thinner and lighter, lower wind ratings, shorter lifespan, and shorter warranties. Many manufacturers have reduced or discontinued their 3-tab lines.

Architectural Shingles

Architectural shingles are made from two or more layers laminated together, creating a thicker shingle with a dimensional, textured look similar to wood shakes or slate.

  • Pros: heavier and more durable, higher wind ratings, longer lifespan, better warranties, and more curb appeal.
  • Cons: costs more than 3-tab, though the difference is often smaller than people expect.

Why Most Houston Homeowners Choose Architectural

Houston roofs face high wind from tropical storms and severe thunderstorms. Architectural shingles' higher wind ratings and heavier construction make them a better match for local weather. They also last longer in heat and usually improve curb appeal and resale value. Many HOAs now require them.
