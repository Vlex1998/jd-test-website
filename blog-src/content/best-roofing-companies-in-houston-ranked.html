What "Best" Should Actually Mean

  • Verified credentials: real manufacturer certifications, not just a logo on a website.
  • A real local track record: years in business, number of completed projects, and reviews you can actually check.
  • Transparent pricing and contracts: written estimates with no pressure tactics.
  • A workmanship warranty: coverage on the labor, not just the shingles.
  • Responsiveness after the job is done: a company that still answers the phone a year later.

The Criteria That Matter Most

  • Licensing and insurance status
  • Manufacturer certifications (GAF, CertainTeed, Owens Corning)
  • Google rating and review volume
  • Years in business and number of completed roofs
  • Whether they're family-owned and locally based, or a franchise/national roll-up
