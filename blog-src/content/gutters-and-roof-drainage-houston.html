Houston rain does not usually fall gently. It comes in heavy bursts that dump a lot of water on your roof in a short time. Your gutters and downspouts are what move that water safely away from your home. When they do not work, the roof, walls, and foundation all pay the price.

How Gutters Protect Your Roof

Water runs down the roof, over the drip edge, and into the gutter. From there, downspouts carry it away from the house. When gutters are clogged or sagging, water backs up and spills over the edge. It can soak the fascia boards, wick under the first row of shingles, and rot the roof decking along the eaves.

Common Gutter Problems in Houston

  • Clogs from leaves, pine needles, oak tassels, and shingle granules.
  • Sagging from loose hangers or the weight of standing water and debris.
  • Leaking seams where gutter sections join.
  • Wrong slope, so water sits in the gutter instead of draining.
  • Rotted fascia behind the gutter, which loosens the whole system.
  • Undersized gutters that cannot keep up with heavy rain, especially below large roof sections or valleys.

The Role of Drip Edge

Drip edge is the metal strip along the roof edge that directs water into the gutter, instead of letting it run behind the gutter onto the fascia. If your roof does not have it, or it was installed incorrectly, water damage along the eaves is common. See our drip edge flashing guide.

Downspouts and Your Foundation

Downspouts should carry water several feet away from the house. Water dumped right at the foundation can cause soil movement, which is a major concern with the expansive clay soils found in much of the Houston area. Extensions, splash blocks, or underground drains help.

Gutter Maintenance Tips

  • Clean gutters at least twice a year, more often if you have trees nearby.
  • Check that water flows freely out of each downspout during a rain.
  • Look for water stains or peeling paint on fascia boards.
  • Consider gutter guards if you have heavy tree cover, but know they still need occasional cleaning.
  • Watch for granules in the gutters. Lots of granules can mean your shingles are wearing out.

Gutters and Roof Replacement

A roof replacement is a good time to check your gutters. Gutters often need to be loosened during the work, and rotted fascia can be replaced while the edge is exposed. If your gutters are old or undersized, replacing them at the same time can save on labor.
