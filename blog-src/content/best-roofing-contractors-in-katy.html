Katy homeowners deal with the same Gulf Coast heat, humidity, and hail that the rest of the Houston metro sees, plus rapid new construction that means a lot of roofs are hitting their first major repair or replacement decision around the same time. Here's what to look for in a roofing contractor serving Katy specifically.

What Matters for Katy Homeowners

  • HOA-friendly documentation: many Katy neighborhoods have HOA architectural approval requirements for roof color and material changes — a good contractor will help you navigate that paperwork.
  • Familiarity with newer-construction roofs: Katy's rapid growth means a lot of roofing work involves builder-grade materials reaching the end of their original warranty.
  • Local response time: a contractor who can actually get a crew to Katy quickly, not just a Houston company that lists it as a service area but rarely shows up.
  • Storm and hail experience: Katy sees its share of severe hail events, so insurance claim experience matters.

How to Evaluate Any Contractor Serving Katy

  • Confirm they're licensed, insured, and carry manufacturer certifications
  • Ask how many Katy-area projects they've actually completed
  • Check recent Google reviews specifically from Katy customers
  • Get a written estimate before agreeing to anything
