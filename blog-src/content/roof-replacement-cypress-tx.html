Cypress has grown rapidly over the past few decades, with large master-planned communities spreading across northwest Harris County. Many of those homes still have their original roofs, and many of those roofs are reaching the end of their life. Here is what Cypress homeowners should know.

Builder-Grade Roofs Are Aging Out

Homes built during big construction booms were often finished with basic, builder-grade shingles. Those roofs can start showing their age after 15 to 20 years in the Texas climate. If your Cypress home is in that range, watch for granule loss, curling shingles, and recurring leaks. See 10 signs you need a new roof.

Hail in Northwest Harris County

Northwest Houston, including Cypress and Katy, sees damaging hail in some spring seasons. Hail damage is often invisible from the ground but shortens a roof's life. After any significant hailstorm, check gutters and vents for dents and schedule an inspection. See how to spot hail damage.

Consider Impact-Resistant Shingles

Because hail is a real risk in this area, Class 4 impact-resistant shingles are worth considering. Many Texas insurers offer premium discounts for them. Ask your agent for the numbers.

HOA Approval

Most Cypress communities have HOAs with roofing guidelines. Plan for the approval process before scheduling installation. See HOA rules and roof replacement.

Upgrade While You Replace

A replacement is the perfect time to fix problems the original builder may have skipped:

  • Improve attic ventilation. See roof ventilation.
  • Replace pipe boots and flashing with quality materials.
  • Upgrade to synthetic underlayment.
  • Add radiant barrier decking where decking needs replacing. See energy-efficient roofing.

Insurance and Storm Chasers

After hailstorms, Cypress neighborhoods often see a wave of out-of-town contractors. Choose a local roofer who will be around to honor the warranty. See local roofer vs storm chaser, and follow our insurance claim guide if you are filing.
