Brown roofs, whether Weathered Wood, Barkwood, or Hickory shingles, are some of the most versatile to design around, since brown reads as a neutral, earthy base rather than a bold statement color.

Exterior Paint Colors That Pair Well

  • Warm white or cream: a classic, high-contrast combination that lets the roof stand out without clashing.
  • Sage or olive green: plays off the roof's warm brown undertones for a natural, grounded look.
  • Soft blue-gray: a cooler contrast that keeps a brown roof from feeling too monochromatic.
  • Taupe or greige: stays in the same warm neutral family for a cohesive, understated exterior.
  • Terracotta or warm clay (trim/accents): works well as an accent color rather than a main body color, echoing the roof's warmth.

Trim and Door Colors

  • Crisp white or off-white trim keeps a brown roof looking clean and defined
  • A dark bronze or black front door adds contrast without competing with the roofline
  • Natural wood-tone garage doors can echo the roof color for a cohesive look

Colors to Use Carefully

Very cool-toned grays or stark blues can sometimes clash with a warm brown roof rather than complement it — if you're set on a cooler palette, look for a brown shingle with more neutral undertones, like Driftwood, rather than a warm Barkwood or Hickory shade.
