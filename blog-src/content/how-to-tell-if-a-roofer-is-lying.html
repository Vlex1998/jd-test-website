Roofing is unfortunately an industry where a small number of bad actors take advantage of homeowners, especially after storms. Here are the warning signs worth knowing before you sign anything.

Red Flags During the Sales Pitch

  • "Your insurance already approved this" before an adjuster has even inspected your roof
  • Pressure to sign the same day, especially with a "today only" discount
  • Vague, verbal-only pricing with nothing written down
  • A stranger who showed up unsolicited right after a storm, with no local presence you can verify

Red Flags in the Contract or Payment Terms

  • Requests for full payment upfront, before any work begins
  • No written scope of work, materials list, or timeline
  • No mention of a workmanship warranty, only the manufacturer's material warranty
  • Reluctance to provide proof of insurance or certification when asked directly

Red Flags During the Job

  • A crew that shows up in unmarked vehicles with no company identification
  • Sudden claims of "extra damage found" with a significantly higher price and no photos to back it up
  • Reluctance to let you inspect the work before final payment

How to Protect Yourself

  • Get everything in writing, including verbal promises
  • Ask for proof of insurance and manufacturer certification directly, and verify it
  • Check recent Google reviews, not just testimonials on the company's own website
  • Never pay the full amount before the job is complete and inspected
