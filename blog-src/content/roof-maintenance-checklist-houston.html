A roof does not need much attention, but it does need some. A few simple checks each season can catch small problems early and add years to its life. Here is a maintenance checklist built around Houston's weather.

Stay off the roof. Almost everything below can be done from the ground, a window, or the attic. Leave anything that requires walking on the roof to a professional.

Spring (March to May)

Spring brings severe thunderstorms and hail to the Houston area.

  • Check for winter damage: loose shingles, sagging gutters, or new ceiling stains.
  • Clean gutters of leaves and oak pollen tassels.
  • After any hailstorm, look for dents in gutters and vents. See how to spot hail damage.
  • Trim branches before storm season.

Early Summer (June)

Hurricane season starts June 1.

  • Schedule a professional inspection if you have not had one in the last year.
  • Review your insurance policy and deductible.
  • Take "before" photos of your roof.
  • Use our hurricane season roof checklist.

Summer (July to August)

Peak heat is hard on shingles and attics.

  • Check the attic on a hot afternoon. Extreme heat or a musty smell can point to ventilation issues. See roof ventilation.
  • Make sure soffit vents are not blocked.
  • Look for curling or blistering shingles.

Fall (September to November)

  • Clean gutters again as leaves and pine needles fall.
  • Check for any storm damage from late hurricane season.
  • Look at caulking and sealant around vents and flashing for cracks.

Winter (December to February)

  • Before a freeze, insulate attic plumbing lines.
  • After a freeze, check for gutter damage and attic condensation. See can a freeze damage your roof?
  • This is a good time to schedule repairs, as roofers are often less busy.

Anytime After a Major Storm

  1. Walk around the house and look for missing shingles or debris.
  2. Check ceilings and the attic for new water stains.
  3. Photograph any damage.
  4. Call for an inspection if anything looks off.

Things to Watch Year-Round

  • Granules collecting in gutters or at downspouts
  • Cracked pipe boots around plumbing vents
  • Black streaks or moss
  • Any change in how the roof looks from the street
