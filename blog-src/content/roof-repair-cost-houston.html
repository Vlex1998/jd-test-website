Roof repair costs in Houston range from a couple hundred dollars for a single pipe boot to several thousand for a storm patch on a steep roof with decking damage. The price depends far more on what is wrong and where it is than on the size of your house. Here are realistic ranges for the repairs we do most, what drives the price up or down, and the point where repairing stops making sense.

Typical Houston Roof Repair Price Ranges

  • Pipe boot replacement: $150 to $400 each. The most common leak fix in Houston. See pipe boot leaks.
  • Replacing a few blown-off or damaged shingles: $250 to $600, depending on access and whether matching shingles are available.
  • Resealing or replacing a roof vent or exhaust cap: $200 to $500.
  • Chimney flashing repair: $400 to $1,500. Full reflash with new step and counter flashing is at the top of that range. See chimney flashing leaks.
  • Skylight reflash or replacement: $500 to $2,000 depending on the unit. See why skylights leak.
  • Valley repair: $500 to $1,500.
  • Storm damage patch (one area of torn-off shingles, new underlayment, possibly a sheet of decking): $600 to $2,500.
  • Decking replacement: $75 to $150 per 4x8 sheet, on top of the repair above it.
  • Flat roof seam or blister repair: $400 to $1,500.
  • Emergency tarp: $300 to $900 depending on size and access. See emergency roof tarping.

These are ballpark 2026 figures for the Houston market. Your estimate will be specific to the roof.

What Makes a Repair Cost More

  • Pitch and height. A 12:12 roof or a third-story roof needs more safety equipment and time.
  • Access. Tight lots, pool enclosures, and landscaping that blocks ladder placement.
  • Matching shingles. Discontinued colors mean either a close match or a larger repair to make it look right.
  • Hidden damage. The leak you see at a pipe boot may have been rotting the decking around it for two years.
  • Tile, slate, or metal. Specialty materials take more skill and more time than asphalt.
  • Minimum service charge. Most companies have a minimum trip charge, often $200 to $350, because a crew and a truck are coming out regardless of how small the fix is.

Diagnosing Before Pricing

A good repair quote starts with finding the actual source of the leak, not just the spot where water shows up inside. Water travels along rafters and underlayment and can enter ten feet from the stain. Read how to find a roof leak. Be wary of a repair quote given from the driveway.

When Repair Stops Making Sense

A repair is the right call when the roof is in generally good shape and the problem is localized. It stops making sense when:

  • The roof is at or past its expected life and the repair is a first of many. See how long a roof lasts in Texas.
  • Shingles are brittle enough that lifting them for the repair cracks the ones around it.
  • You are on your third or fourth repair in two years.
  • The repair total approaches 25 to 30 percent of the replacement cost.
  • Storm damage is spread across multiple slopes and insurance may cover a replacement.

Does Insurance Pay for Repairs?

Only if the damage came from a covered event like wind or hail, and only above your deductible. Most Texas wind and hail deductibles are 1 to 2 percent of the home's insured value, which makes small repairs an out-of-pocket expense. See does insurance cover roof leaks.
