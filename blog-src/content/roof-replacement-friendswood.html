Friendswood is split between Harris and Galveston counties, which means two neighbors on the same street can have different permit offices, different windstorm insurance situations, and different roofing requirements. Add heavy Gulf rain, coastal wind, and some of the strictest HOAs in the south Houston area, and roof replacement here takes some planning.

Which County Are You In?

Most of Friendswood is in Galveston County. The northern tip, roughly north of Clear Creek, is in Harris County. Why it matters:

  • Galveston County homes are in the Texas Windstorm Insurance Association catastrophe area. Many carry wind and hail coverage through TWIA and need a WPI-8 windstorm certificate on new roof work to remain insurable.
  • Harris County homes west of Highway 146 are outside the TWIA zone and usually carry wind coverage through their regular homeowners policy.

The WPI-8 process requires an inspection during the installation, before the shingles go on. Our League City and Clear Lake guide explains it in detail. Ask any roofer you interview whether they handle windstorm inspections.

Wind and Rain

Friendswood is about 25 miles from the Gulf. Tropical storms arrive with strong wind and the kind of rain that overwhelms gutters in minutes. Harvey dropped more than 40 inches here. A roof in this town should be installed for wind first and drainage second:

  • Six-nail high-wind shingle installation with starter strip at eaves and rakes
  • Sealed deck seams and full synthetic underlayment
  • Ice and water shield in valleys and around all penetrations
  • Oversized gutters and downspouts on larger roofs. See gutters and roof drainage.

HOA Color and Material Rules

West Ranch, Friendswood Lakes, Eagle Lakes, Autumn Creek, and most newer Friendswood communities have architectural review with specific approved shingle colors and often a dimensional-shingle requirement. Older neighborhoods like Imperial Estates and Wedgewood are more relaxed. Texas law prevents HOAs from banning impact-resistant or energy-efficient shingles that look like approved products. See HOA rules and roof replacement in Texas.

Permits

The City of Friendswood requires a permit for roof replacement regardless of which county the home is in. The contractor should be registered with the city and should pull the permit. If you are in the Galveston County portion, the windstorm inspection is a separate step from the city permit.

Materials That Make Sense Here

  • Architectural shingles with a 130 mph rating are the standard choice and are accepted by every HOA in town.
  • Class 4 impact-resistant shingles earn insurance discounts and hold up to hail, which Friendswood does get.
  • Metal is a good option on larger lots and for homeowners who want a 40-plus year roof. It must be a windstorm-approved product if you are in the TWIA zone. See metal roofing in Texas.

Insurance Claims

If your wind coverage is through TWIA, the claim goes to TWIA and follows its rules. If it is through a standard carrier, our Texas roof insurance claim guide walks through the process. In both cases, document damage quickly and do not sign a contract with the first crew that knocks.
