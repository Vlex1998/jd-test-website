This is one of the most common questions Houston homeowners ask us, and the honest answer depends on a handful of specific factors rather than a single rule of thumb.

Signs a Repair Is Usually Enough

  • Damage is isolated to a small area (a single leak, a few missing shingles, damaged flashing)
  • The roof is less than 10–15 years old and otherwise in good condition
  • This is the first repair the roof has needed

Signs You Should Consider Replacement

  • The roof is approaching or past its expected lifespan for its material
  • You've needed multiple repairs in the last few years
  • Damage is widespread rather than isolated (major hail impact, storm damage across large sections)
  • There's granule loss, curling, or cracking across most of the roof, not just one spot
  • You're seeing daylight through the roof deck or recurring leaks in different spots

Other Factors to Weigh

  • Cost over time: repeated repairs on an aging roof can add up to more than a full replacement over a few years.
  • Insurance: if damage is storm-related, a full replacement may be covered where a patch repair wouldn't fully address the underlying issue.
  • Resale plans: a new roof is one of the highest-return improvements if you're planning to sell in the next few years.
  • Energy efficiency: newer shingle technology can improve attic temperatures and cooling costs in Houston's climate.
