Commercial Systems

Roofing Systems We Install

We work with the top commercial roofing systems to match every building type, budget, and performance requirement.

TPO Roofing

Thermoplastic Polyolefin (TPO) is one of the fastest-growing commercial roofing membranes. It offers exceptional UV and heat resistance — perfect for Houston's intense summers — along with energy efficiency and long service life.

EPDM Roofing

Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer (EPDM) is a highly durable synthetic rubber membrane ideal for flat and low-slope commercial roofs. It resists weathering, ozone, and UV exposure with a proven 20+ year lifespan.

Modified Bitumen

Modified bitumen combines traditional asphalt with polymer reinforcement for added strength and flexibility. It's an excellent choice for flat roofs that need to handle heavy foot traffic and extreme temperature swings.

Built-Up Roofing (BUR)

Built-up roofing has been a commercial standard for over 100 years. Multiple layers of bitumen and reinforcing fabric create a waterproof membrane with outstanding durability and fire resistance for large flat roof applications.

Roof Coatings

Reflective roof coatings extend the life of an existing commercial roof by 10–15 years while dramatically reducing cooling costs. A cost-effective alternative to full replacement when the structure is still sound.

Metal Roofing

Standing seam metal roofing is a premium commercial solution with a 40–60 year lifespan. It's ideal for warehouses, industrial facilities, and high-end commercial buildings that demand maximum longevity.

FAQ

Commercial Roofing Questions

What is the best roofing system for a flat commercial roof? +

For Houston's climate, TPO is often the best choice — its white reflective surface reduces cooling costs significantly. EPDM is excellent for durability and longevity. Modified bitumen works well for roofs with heavy foot traffic. We'll inspect your property and recommend the right system for your specific building type and budget.

How long does a commercial roof installation take? +

Timeline depends on the roof size and system type. Small commercial roofs (under 10,000 sq ft) are typically completed in 2–5 days. Larger facilities may require 1–3 weeks. We provide a firm schedule upfront and stick to it — no open-ended projects.

Will my business need to close during the roof replacement? +

In most cases, no. We can sequence the work to minimize interior disruptions. For sensitive operations, we can schedule work nights and weekends. We'll coordinate with your facility manager to find the schedule that causes the least impact to your business.

Does commercial roof damage qualify for an insurance claim? +

Yes. Commercial properties are covered by commercial property insurance for storm, hail, and wind damage. We work with commercial insurance adjusters regularly. Our team can document the damage, prepare a detailed estimate, and help guide your adjuster toward an accurate assessment.

How often should a commercial roof be inspected? +

We recommend commercial roof inspections twice per year — once in spring after hail season risk and once in fall before winter weather. After any major storm event, an additional inspection is wise. Regular inspections extend roof life, keep warranties valid, and catch small issues before they become expensive emergencies.
