Roof Replacement in Spring, TX: What Homeowners Should Know

JD Roofing Solutions · October 5, 2026 · 2 min read

Spring and the Klein area just north of Houston have a mix of established neighborhoods with mature trees and newer subdivisions. JD Roofing Solutions is based on Veterans Memorial Drive in north Houston, just minutes from Spring, so we see the roofing issues common here every week.

Aging Roofs in Established Neighborhoods

Many homes in Spring and Klein were built in the 1980s, 1990s, and 2000s. Some are on their second roof, and some of those are wearing out too. Common signs include granule loss, curling shingles, cracked pipe boots, and leaks around chimneys and walls. See 10 signs you need a new roof.

Trees and Roofs

Mature pines and oaks are part of what makes Spring neighborhoods attractive. They also drop needles and leaves that clog valleys and gutters, shade roofs into algae growth, and can drop limbs in storms. See overhanging trees and your roof.

Storm Exposure

Spring sees severe thunderstorms with high wind and occasional hail, plus tropical systems in hurricane season. Inspect your roof after major storms and before hurricane season. See our hurricane season checklist.

Repair or Replace?

If your roof is relatively young and the problem is limited, such as a cracked pipe boot or a few missing shingles, a repair is usually the right call. If the roof is older and has multiple issues, replacement may cost less over time. See should I repair or replace my roof?

Permits and HOAs

Requirements depend on whether your home is inside city limits or in unincorporated Harris or Montgomery County, and on your neighborhood's HOA rules. Your roofer should know what applies to your address. See HOA rules and roof replacement.

What a Good Spring Roof Replacement Includes

  • Full tear-off down to the decking, with damaged decking replaced
  • Synthetic underlayment and ice and water shield in valleys and around penetrations
  • New drip edge, flashing, and pipe boots
  • Algae-resistant architectural or impact-resistant shingles
  • Balanced attic ventilation
  • Thorough cleanup and nail sweep
