System Breakdown

Anatomy of Your Roofing System

See every layer that protects your home — from the rafters to the final shingle course.

1

Roof Ridge

Horizontal peak where two roof planes meet

2

Ridge Vent

Exhaust vent along the peak for attic airflow

4

Hip

Sloping ridge where two roof planes intersect

5

Roof Deck

Structural plywood/OSB sheathing base

6

Roofing Underlayment

Synthetic or felt moisture barrier under shingles

7

Roof Valley

V-shaped channel where two slopes meet

8

Architectural Shingles

Laminated shingles — primary weather layer

9

Roof Gable

Triangular wall section at the end of a roof

10

Metal Drip Edge

Directs water runoff away from fascia

12

Ice & Water Barrier

Self-adhering waterproofing at eaves & valleys

13

Eave

Lower roof edge that overhangs the wall

14

Undereave Vent

Intake vent drawing cool air into the attic
