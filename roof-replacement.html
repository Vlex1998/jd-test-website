Warning Signs

Does Your Roof Need Replacing?

Catching these signs early can save you thousands. Here's what to look for.

Hail & Storm Damage

Hailstorms can compromise roof integrity. If you notice dents, missing granules, or cracked shingles after a storm, a full replacement may be needed.

Active Leaks

A leaky roof can lead to serious structural damage, mold, and rot if left unaddressed. Water stains on ceilings or walls are urgent warning signs.

Missing Shingles

Missing shingles expose your home to the elements. If large sections are bare or damaged, replacement is more cost-effective than repeated repairs.

Age (20+ Years)

Most asphalt shingle roofs last 20–25 years. If yours is approaching that age, proactive replacement prevents emergencies and protects your investment.

Granule Loss

If you're finding granules in your gutters, your shingles are deteriorating. This UV protection loss accelerates roof aging significantly.

High Energy Bills

A failing roof loses insulating efficiency. If your energy bills have spiked, a new roof with modern materials can dramatically reduce heating and cooling costs.

FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a roof replacement take? +

Most residential roof replacements are completed in one day. Larger or more complex roofs may take 2–3 days. We give you a firm timeline before starting.

Will my insurance cover a roof replacement? +

If your roof was damaged by a storm, hail, or wind, your homeowner's insurance will likely cover the replacement minus your deductible. We work directly with insurance adjusters and handle the entire claims process for you.

What materials do you use? +

We are a GAF Certified Contractor and use premium asphalt shingles, synthetic underlayment, and high-grade flashing. We also offer metal, tile, and flat roof systems depending on your needs.

Do you offer financing? +

Yes! We offer flexible financing through Wisetack. Most customers can start their project with low monthly payments. Ask us about current rates during your free estimate.

What warranty do you provide? +

As a GAF Certified Contractor, we offer manufacturer warranties on materials plus a workmanship warranty on our installation. We'll walk you through all warranty details before the job begins.
