What We Inspect

Our inspectors check every component of your roofing system — not just the shingles.

Shingles & Surface

Missing, cracked, curled, or blistered shingles — and granule loss from the surface.

Flashing & Seals

Chimney, vent, and valley flashing — the most common source of roof leaks.

Gutters & Drainage

Clogged gutters cause overflow and foundation erosion. We check flow and attachment.

Attic & Ventilation

Wet insulation, dark stains, mold — signs of hidden leaks that only attic access reveals.

Annual Maintenance

4 Essential Yearly Checks

We recommend these four maintenance steps every year — especially after Houston's storm season.

1

Gutter Inspection

Clogged gutters lead to water overflow that damages fascia, soffits, and your foundation. We clear blockages and check for proper pitch and secure attachment.

2

Attic Inspection

We check for wet insulation, dark stains, or visible mold — signs of active leaks that aren't visible from outside. Attic access often reveals problems months before interior damage appears.

3

Skylight & Vent Maintenance

Debris accumulation around skylights and vents causes scratches, discoloration, and potential leaks. Regular cleaning and seal inspection prevents costly water intrusion.

4

Roof Surface Inspection

Using binoculars from ground level, we identify missing shingles and compromised flashing around chimneys and vents — without putting unsafe foot traffic on your roof.

FAQ

Inspection Questions

Is the inspection really free?

Yes — completely free, no strings attached. We inspect your roof, document findings, and give you a detailed written report. You decide what to do next with zero pressure.

How long does an inspection take?

Most residential inspections take 30–60 minutes depending on roof size and complexity. We're thorough — we don't rush through it.

How often should I have my roof inspected?

We recommend annual inspections for all Houston homeowners, plus an inspection after any significant storm or hail event. Houston's weather is particularly hard on roofs.

Can you inspect my roof for an insurance claim?

Absolutely. Our inspection report is thorough enough to support an insurance claim, and we handle the entire claims process for you at no extra charge.
